#### Welcome to PrimSys!

PrimSys is a passion project of mine, written primarily with **JSON** and **C#**, with a small amount of **C++**.

The goal of PrimSys is to create a **small, portable, and modular system** that provides basic functionality while remaining fully open source. PrimSys is intended to be a project that can grow alongside its community, with anyone welcome to contribute.

Contributions can take many forms, whether that means developing additional modules, implementing new subprocesses, improving existing functionality, or even helping PrimSys evolve into a fully-fledged and respectable operating system.

This documentation contains everything you need to understand and work with PrimSys, including its **codebase, architecture, implementation, compilation process, and user manual.**

Whether you are interested in exploring the project, contributing code, experimenting with its architecture, or simply learning how PrimSys works, this documentation is the starting point.

**Welcome to PrimSys.**
